## 6. Adaptive prices the habit, not the verb, and rotation is the intended answer.
Phase 4 shipped Adaptive as the paired constraint to Vengeful, picked by `enemy_constraint` and never running on the same enemy. It reads the only discretionary choices this build has, whether you swing and when, because the counter is dictated by the enemy's attack and so carries no repetition worth punishing. A signature is a swing plus the enemy's state at the moment of the press: `GUARD` (IDLE, the guard beat), `WINDUP`, `AFTER` (STRIKE, RECOVERY, or the stance shift), and `PATIENCE` (no accepted swing anywhere in the cycle). It is the choice and not the outcome, so a press that whiffs still records. STRIKE and RECOVERY are the tail of the cycle that just telegraphed; the stance shift is not, since it begins after the boundary has already counted, so a press during the lift is credited to the FOLLOWING cycle. It shares AFTER anyway, because a press belonging to no bucket would silently read as PATIENCE.
Counting is a per-cycle seen-set: a press only marks its bucket, and once at the recovery exit each marked bucket gains 1.0 while every unmarked one decays to 40% of what it held. One cycle is worth at most one point to any one bucket, so `adaptive_threshold` N means N consecutive cycles out of a COLD bucket. Out of a warm one it is fewer: activation tests the accumulated weight, not a fresh run, so k consecutive cycles cross once k >= N - 0.4 * w_prev, and a duty cycle fatter than one-in-p is caught too. Swinging twice and resting once in one bucket at threshold 3 runs 1, 2, 0.8, 1.8, 2.8, 1.12, 2.12, 3.12 and adapts off the last TWO consecutive cycles, which is wanted, that is still a habit. What is never caught is a STRICT rotation of period p, which settles at 1 / (1 - 0.4^(p-1)): alternating two buckets tops out at 1.67 and rotating three tops out at 1.19, both under any threshold of 2 or more permanently. The formula bounds the rotations and says nothing about a fatter duty cycle.
Eviction is what makes rotation an answer rather than a delay: an adaptation never expires on its own, decay does not flush it, and at `adaptive_capacity` 2 a new one pops the OLDEST and zeroes its weight. Getting an adaptation off you costs building a different one, and the evicted bucket then has to be re-earned from zero.
Conclusion: aggression is dominant and then priced, instead of dominant outright as findings 3 recorded. A wind-up cancel still works and still buys about 2.9-3.1 s against a 1.0 s lockout, for exactly `adaptive_threshold` cycles out of a COLD `WINDUP` bucket and for fewer out of a warm one, since learning is not cleared by death or by FIGHT WON and a second fight can start warm and adapt sooner. Then the `WINDUP` bucket adapts and the same press stops working. That line is the pure wind-up cancel, one HP a cycle, so at threshold 3 it stops with 3 of the enemy's 6 HP still up; the two-HP-a-cycle line is a counter plus a poke into the recovery, and it adapts AFTER instead. `PATIENCE` closes the other half by compressing the guard beat and its jitter to `adaptive_patience_guard_multiplier` of full length, so the counter-only line from findings 4 is not free either.
Open: whether the player ever changes their swing timing SPECIFICALLY because the enemy learned the old one is UNMEASURED, and it is the whole question of the phase. Also UNMEASURED: whether a run of whiffs is legible enough to infer WHICH bucket was learned, since the HUD deliberately refuses to say and prints only an anonymous active count; whether the 0.28 s compressed beat still reads as a beat at the shipped 0.5 multiplier; and whether rotating buckets is a choice the player notices making or something their hands were already doing. No Godot binary on this machine (PATH, Program Files and the usual per-user install roots all probed), so Adaptive ships reviewed-not-runtime-confirmed like Phases 2 and 3. Four things have been reasoned about and none have been seen running: the stance shift being readable, an adapted swing visibly whiffing, varying timing actually flushing an adaptation, and pure patience no longer being free. The only executable check is an assert-based `_selftest_adaptive()` called from `_ready()`, over the weight arithmetic, the PATIENCE derivation, and eviction at both one and two pops.
